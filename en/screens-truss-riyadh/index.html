FAQ About Screens Rental

How much does display screens rental in Riyadh cost?

The price depends on the screen size, the type of use, installation location and rental period, and truss gates are priced by size and design. Send us the details on WhatsApp and we will send a written quote including installation and operation.

What do I need to run the screen at my venue?

You need a power source near the installation spot and your content ready (the match channel or the presentation files). We handle installation, connection and operation, and make sure the picture is clear from all seating areas before we leave.

Do screens suit outdoor venues?

Yes, in roofed or shaded venues, and we advise installing them away from direct sunlight at show time so the picture is as clear as possible, especially for daytime shows.

Can I book a screen and a truss gate together?

Yes, and it is common at events: a truss gate at the entrance carrying the occasion’s name and a display screen inside the venue, in one installation and one bundle.
