FAQ About Coffee Servers

How many Qahwajis and servers do I need for my event?

As a practical rule: a Qahwaji and a server for roughly every 50 guests at medium events, and the ratio rises at large weddings so service never stops. Tell us your guest count and event type and we will suggest the right number without exaggeration.

Are female servers available for the women’s section?

Yes, we provide female servers for the women’s section and Qahwajis and male servers for the men’s section, with neat, organised presence suited to each occasion.

How much does Qahwaji and server service in Riyadh cost?

The price depends on the team size, service duration and event location. Send us the details on WhatsApp and we will send a clear written quote before booking.

What does the hospitality service include?

Running the Arabic coffee and tea service throughout your event in neat attire with organised presence, coordinated with you on serving times and seating areas. If you need extra equipment such as seating and tables, we add it to your bundle.
