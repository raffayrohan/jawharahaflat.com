FAQ About Wedding Koshas

When should I book the Kosha before the wedding?

Best is two to four weeks before the wedding, especially in wedding and holiday seasons, so we can secure the design and colours you want. Close bookings are possible whenever capacity allows.

Is the Kosha designed to my wedding theme and colours?

Yes, that is the basis of our work: send us your wedding colours or reference photos you like, and we will build the Kosha, entrance and styling in the same spirit, with suggestions from us if you are undecided.

How much does a wedding Kosha setup in Riyadh cost?

The price depends on the Kosha size and the bundle elements (entrance, guest styling, lighting) and the installation location. Send us the date and venue on WhatsApp and we will send a written, itemised quote before any commitment.

Does the bundle include guest sofas, chairs and tables?

You can add them all in the same bundle: sofas, chairs and tables in one style consistent with the Kosha, in one delivery and installation, at a better price than ordering each service alone.
