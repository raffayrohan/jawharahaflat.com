Winter Kashta & Outdoor Sessions in Riyadh: The Complete Setup Guide

From November to February, Riyadh lives its finest season: cold nights, fire and wood, and a group gathered around a well-set session. This guide equips your Kashta item by item, from the seating type to the game that stretches the night.

First: Seating Is the Heart of the Kashta

Choosing the wrong seating spoils the finest winter night. The difference between types is not cosmetic; it depends on the ground and the group's nature:

  • Floor seating: closest to the spirit of the Kashta and the desert; mats, carpets and backrests right around the fire. Ideal for camps and open land in Al Thumama and around Riyadh.
  • Moroccan seating: floor seating with a luxurious feel and high, comfortable backrests for long sitting; suits rest houses and anyone who wants the Kashta with a tidier touch.
  • Merkaz and raised seating: for those who prefer sitting higher off the ground; suits paved yards and equipped rest houses.

Whichever type you choose, insist on clean carpets covering the whole seating area; a missing or dirty carpet is the first thing guests notice in floor seating specifically, because everything is close to the eye.

Second: The Games That Stretch the Night

A successful Kashta has two activities: a session around the fire, and a game that gathers whoever wants movement and competition. From rest house experience, these are the most requested combinations:

  • Baloot & Carrom: winter's first choice; needs little space and the whole session revolves around it.
  • Billiards: for rest houses with a hall or covered yard; a billiards tournament between the group becomes the talk of the whole week.
  • Foosball & table tennis: fast-paced games that suit the youth and install in any wide corner.

A tested tip: two games are enough for any Kashta, however large the group. A third game usually stays without players and crowds the space. Pick one calm game (Baloot) and one energetic game (billiards or foosball) and you will cover every taste.

Third: Lighting & Hospitality After Sunset

The winter night truly starts after sunset, and here the difference shows between an ordinary Kashta and one people talk about:

  • Warm ambient lighting around the seating, enough to see without killing the fire's atmosphere, plus a spotlight aimed at the games area.
  • A coffee server (Qahwaji) running coffee and tea all night; in winter the dallah circulates more than any season, and having someone run hospitality frees you for your guests.
  • A display screen if the night meets an important match; half the group came for it anyway.

The Complete Kashta Setup Checklist

  1. Seating (floor, Moroccan or Merkaz) sized for the group, with a margin.
  2. Clean carpets and mats covering the full seating area.
  3. A Baloot or Carrom table with its full kit.
  4. One energetic game table: billiards, foosball or table tennis.
  5. Lighting for the seating and the games area.
  6. Hospitality: a coffee server for coffee and tea.
  7. A display screen when needed (matches and occasions).

All of these items arrive with us in one delivery and are installed before the group arrives, then collected after the night ends or the next day as agreed, whether your Kashta is at a rest house inside Riyadh or a camp in Al Thumama or Al Amariyah.

FAQ About Winter Kashtas

What is the best seating for a winter Kashta?

Floor and Moroccan seating is the first choice for Kashtas because it sits close to the fire and gathers the group in one circle. Merkaz and raised seating suit rest houses and paved yards, and anyone who prefers sitting higher off the ground.

Do you deliver Kashta equipment to rest houses outside Riyadh?

Yes, we deliver and install at rest houses and camps on the edges of Riyadh such as Al Thumama and Al Amariyah, including seating, game tables and lighting. Message us with the location and date and we will confirm delivery and the full price.

When does Kashta season start in Riyadh, and when should I book?

The season effectively starts in November and peaks in December and January. On winter weekends, seating and games are booked early, so book a few days before your weekend, especially if you want a bundle of seating with billiards or Baloot.
