FAQ About Lighting Rental

How much does event lighting rental in Riyadh cost?

The price depends on the lighting types, the number of units and the venue size. Send us your event type and space on WhatsApp and we will recommend a suitable lighting plan with a written quote including wiring and installation.

How many spotlights or lighting units do I need?

It depends on the area, the venue height and the event type. Describe your place or send a photo of it, and we will calculate the right distribution with no extra units to pay for.

Does the service include wiring and installation?

Yes, we run the cables, install and fix the units safely and test the full lighting before the event, then dismantle and collect everything afterwards.

Does the lighting suit outdoor venues?

We provide units suited to outdoor use in yards, rest houses and tents, distributed to fit the place and its mood, with cables fixed safely away from guest walkways.
