FAQ About Sofas & Chairs Rental

How much does sofas and chairs rental for events in Riyadh cost?

The price depends on the sofa or chair type, the quantity, delivery location and rental period, and quantity bundles are better value than single pieces. Send us the type, quantity and date on WhatsApp and we will send a written quote including delivery and arrangement.

Can I rent sofas, chairs and tables together in one order?

Yes, and that is what most wedding customers do: sofas, chairs and tables in one consistent style from one place, at a better bundle price, in one delivery, arranged consistently in your event photos.

Are the sofas and chairs clean and photo-ready?

Every piece is cleaned and checked before delivery, because sofas and chairs appear in every wedding photo. If you notice anything on receipt, tell us immediately and we will fix it before the event starts.

How many chairs do I need for my event?

The practical rule: your expected guest count plus a 10% margin for extra visitors. Tell us the guest count and event type and we will suggest the right split between sofas, chairs and tables.
