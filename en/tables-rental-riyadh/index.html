FAQ About Tables Rental

How much does tables rental in Riyadh cost?

The price depends on the table type and count, whether you want full styling with them, and the delivery location. Send us the count, type and date on WhatsApp and we will send a written quote including delivery, arrangement and styling.

How many tables do I need for my guests?

As a practical rule: one round table serves 8 to 10 guests, and a rectangular one 6 to 8. Tell us your guest count and space size and we will calculate the right number with no extras to pay for.

Is styling included with tables rental?

We always arrange the tables in place, and full styling with linens, tableware and candles is an optional add-on to your bundle. Tell us your taste or send a reference photo and we will match it as closely as possible.

Can I rent tables with chairs and sofas in one order?

Yes, and it is the smartest way: one consistent style across tables, chairs and sofas, one delivery, and a better bundle price than ordering each service alone.
