FAQ About Baloot & Carrom Tables

Does the Baloot table arrive with the full playing kit?

Yes, the Baloot table arrives with playing cards, a score board and everything the round needs. The Carrom board arrives with its coins, queen and striker, and chess with its complete set of pieces.

Can I rent Baloot and Carrom tables together?

Yes, most rest house customers order Baloot and Carrom together, and you can add chess or the larger game tables such as billiards and foosball in the same order at a bundle price.

How long is the rental period for Baloot and Carrom tables?

Rental starts from one day and is available for several days or the whole camp or holiday period, subject to availability. The longer the period, the better the daily rate. Message us on WhatsApp with the details.
