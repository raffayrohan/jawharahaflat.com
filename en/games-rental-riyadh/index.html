FAQ About Game Tables Rental

Which game tables are available for rent in Riyadh?

We offer billiards, table tennis, air hockey and foosball tables, plus Baloot, Carrom and chess tables on a dedicated page. Every table arrives with its full playing accessories, ready to play right away.

Are delivery and installation included when renting a billiards table?

Your quote lists everything, including delivery, installation and collection inside Riyadh. A billiards table in particular needs precise installation and levelling, and our team installs and levels it at your venue before the event.

How long can I rent a game table for?

Rental is available for the event day, or for several days or a week for rest houses and camps, subject to availability. Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and duration and we will send the right price.

Can I rent more than one table in a single order?

Yes, you can combine billiards with foosball and table tennis in one order, and a bundle price is usually better than renting each table separately. Ask us about rest house bundles on WhatsApp.
