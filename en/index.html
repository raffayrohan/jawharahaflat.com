Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what our customers ask most before booking.

How do I book equipment for my event?

Message us on WhatsApp with the event type, date, district and the services you need, and we will send a clear quote. Once you confirm, we lock the booking and arrange delivery and installation before your event.

Are delivery and installation included in the price?

Your quote lists every item in detail, including delivery, installation and collection inside Riyadh, so you know the full cost before you confirm. No surprises.

How early should I book before my event?

The earlier the better, especially in wedding and holiday seasons. We also accept short-notice requests whenever equipment is available, so message us on WhatsApp and we will check your date.

Do you serve rest houses and camps outside residential districts?

Yes, we set up rest houses and camps on the edges of Riyadh such as Al Thumama and Al Amariyah, including outdoor seating, game tables and lighting suited to open-air venues.

Is there a minimum order?

The minimum depends on the service type and delivery location. Tell us what you need and we will suggest the best package, whether it is a small home gathering or a full wedding.
